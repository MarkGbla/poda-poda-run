## Congo Cross
Traits
- The junction sits at the foot of the Wilkinson Road/Peace Bridge. The bridge is a dual carriageway with cream concrete balustrade railings (vertical balusters, flat coping) on both sides, grey double-arm street lamps, and red Africell poster boards strapped to the lamp posts. Billboards (Ecobank purple/blue, Gulf Oil green) on the approach.
- Behind the bridge: wooded valley (Congo stream) and hillside houses in cream, pale yellow and white with rust roofs climbing the slope.
- The junction itself: a tiny mini-roundabout with a cream tiled kerb and planted centre, GTCO orange signs, 2-storey commercial blocks with hoardings painted along the ground floor, laterite stone retaining walls banded with blue paint, "Continental / Dunlop" wall ads.
- Rebranded "Peace Bridge" in 2003.

## Cotton Tree
What stands there NOW (2024-2025)
- The tree fell on the night of 24 May 2023. The fallen crown was removed to a museum. What remains is a cluster of three or four charred, jagged trunk spires 8-12 m tall, with green regrowth sprouting from the base and sides (reported healthy in March 2024; the 2025 Wikipedia photo shows leafy regrowth).
- The lower trunk is wrapped in a huge Sierra Leone flag (green / white / blue horizontal bands).
- It sits on a stepped circular plinth of dark stone/tile with a black-and-white painted kerb in the middle of the roundabout.
- No new monument had been built by the time of the 2025 photo; President Bio promised one. *Uncertain* whether a monument has gone up since.
Surroundings
- Law Courts: cream two-storey colonial block with columns and a red tiled roof directly behind the tree. National Museum: small cream building with red roof on the other side.
- NASSIT House: white multi-storey block with a vertical blue stripe. Other mid-rise office blocks (6-10 storeys, cream concrete) ring the roundabout.

## PZ
Traits
- Downtown CBD around Siaka Stevens Street / Rawdon Street. Continuous 3-6 storey frontage: cream and white rendered blocks, concrete brise-soleil grids, balconies, air-con units, painted hoardings and shop boards on the ground floor, telecom billboards (Airtel red, Africell, Orange).
- Sierra Leone Commercial Bank tower: tan/sand brutalist tower (about 12 storeys) with vertical concrete ribs and arched openings at ground level; masts on the roof.
- Rokel Commercial Bank: white building with a blue brise-soleil grid and blue roof trim.
- Rusty three-way street signpost: "SIAKA STEVENS STREET / STATE HOUSE / COMMERCIAL BANK".
- Yellow kekehs, taxis with blue/yellow livery, crowds, wires everywhere.

## Abacha Street
Traits
- Wall-to-wall market. Rainbow segmented umbrellas (red / yellow / green / blue) every few metres, with blue and black tarpaulin ones in between.
- Goods on the ground on tarps and tables: clothes, bags hung on frames, basins, plastic goods, food. Wheelbarrows and head-loads.
- Buildings are older 2-3 storey shophouses: blue-painted shopfronts with hand-painted boards ("S. JALLOH ENTERPRISES", "IBRAHIM MOHSEN AND SONS", "MUSANTA"), wooden first-floor balconies, an orange-and-white striped corner block, Coca-Cola signs, green/white/blue bunting strung between poles.
- Stalls were demolished in Aug 2022 but traders returned; street resurfaced 2023.

## Eastern Police
Traits
- East End Police Clock Tower stands in the middle of the junction (Kissy Road / Fourah Bay Road). A white/cream square tower, roughly 12 m, with a clock face (black Roman numerals on white) on each side, louvred slots below the clocks, a concrete pyramidal cap, and a stepped base.
- Around it: busy East End market, orange, yellow and cream 3-storey blocks with balconies, umbrellas, poda-podas and kekehs.
- A 2026 restoration project is uncovering the tower's original laterite stonework and replacing the clock. *Uncertain*: the finished tower may read as reddish laterite rather than white. The Alamy image labelled "crenellated brick tower" (HDWJFP) does not match the other photos and may be a different tower; ignored.

## FBC (Fourah Bay College, Mount Aureol)
Traits
- Campus sits high on Mount Aureol above the east end, reached by a steep wooded road. Hillside is dense green with mango trees and very tall, narrow dark conifers (cypress/casuarina) lining the campus roads.
- Buildings: cream rendered blocks of 2-4 storeys with dark-green painted bands and trim, a distinctive sawtooth (zig-zag) roofline on the admin block, black water tanks on roofs, lamp posts, clipped hedges.
- From the campus you look down over the harbour and the container port; the city below is brown zinc roofs.
- The famous laterite-red-brick Old Fourah Bay College building with arched windows and cast-iron veranda is at Cline Town (down by the port), not on the hill; it is being restored. Only referenced as a nod.
