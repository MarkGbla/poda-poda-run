# Vehicle reference notes

Reference photos are in `research/refs/vehicles/`. They are for modelling reference only and are not shipped.

## Poda-poda (Mercedes-Benz Sprinter T1N high-roof minibus)
Per the user (a Freetown local), most Freetown poda-podas are first-generation Mercedes Sprinters. The photos below confirm the livery conventions, though some of them show older Toyota, Mazda and Suzuki vans.
- **Silhouette** (`poda-07`, `poda-08`): short sloping bonnet, steeply raked one-piece windscreen and a tall high roof with a raised cap above the windscreen. Long side windows with body-coloured pillars, a sliding door on the right behind the cab door, large corner headlights, a grille with the star, tall vertical tail lights at the rear corners and twin rear doors.
- **Front slogan** (`poda-01`, `poda-02`, `poda-03`): big painted words across the front panel under the windscreen, such as "FEAR JUDGEMENT DAY", "THE LORD IS MY SHEPHERD" and "BLACK MAN TRANSPORT". They are red or blue letters with a dark outline on white. The user says "GOD IS GREAT" is the most common.
- **Paint** (`poda-02`, `poda-04`, `poda-05`): mostly a white or cream base. Common treatments are a red lower skirt with a thin pinstripe, double orange-and-blue pinstripes with a red/blue "flame" swoosh and a black-and-white sawtooth band along the sill (`poda-04`), and a blue-painted lower body on older vans.
- **Side name**: the transport company name painted on the body sides, for example KAMARA TOURS or SESAY TRANSPORT (from the user).
- **Rear**: slogan stickers on the rear window and the route painted below it.
- **Route board**: a destination board or sticker along the top of the windscreen.
- **Roof and crew**: loads on a roof rack (sacks, tarp bundles, jerrycans, boxes). The apprentice hangs out of the open sliding door on the right (`poda-04`, `poda-06`).

## Kekeh (Bajaj RE / TVS King auto-rickshaw)
- **Colours** (`keke-01`, `keke-02`, `keke-05`): yellow and orange-yellow with black trim, solid green, and TVS blue with yellow bumpers.
- **Two-tone** (`keke-03`, `keke-04`): green lower body with a yellow upper body.
- **Canopy**: black canvas with a curved roof. The rear canvas wall has a small clear window and printed text, for example "EXECUTIVE TRANSPORTATION" (`keke-02`).
- **Body** (`keke-01`, `keke-02`): narrow front cowl with a single round headlight, a near-vertical windscreen and mirrors on stalks. Chrome grab rails run along the open sides. Tail lights sit low at the rear corners, above a rear bar.

## Okada (motorbike taxi)
- **Bikes** (`okada-02`, `okada-03`): Bajaj Boxer, TVS and Haojue commuter bikes in red, black or blue, with a chrome rear carrier, a chrome exhaust on the right, a round headlight cowl and tall mirror stalks.
- **Riders** (`okada-01`, `okada-03`, `okada-04`, `okada-05`, `okada-06`): the rider usually wears a helmet (red/white party helmets in `okada-06`, a hi-vis vest in `okada-05`). The passenger rides pillion behind and usually has no helmet.

## Freetown taxi
- **Car** (`taxi-01`): Nissan Sunny (B13/N14) three-box saloon painted taxi yellow, with black bumpers and a thin black waist stripe. Some have a mismatched grey replacement bonnet.
- **Ward lettering** (`taxi-02`): "WEST WARD" lettered on the rear door (East/West ward zoning).

## Waka Fine bus (launched 29 January 2024)
- **Bus and livery** (`wakafine-01`, `wakafine-02`): low-floor city bus in national colours. The roof and the cap over the windscreen are green, the middle is white and the lower skirt is blue with a thin yellow line at the bottom.
- **Lettering**: "WAKA FINE / Moving more people faster" with a bus roundel on a white band under the windows.
- **Windows and doors**: a dark continuous window band, and glass front and middle doors on the right.
- **Front** (`wakafine-03`): an amber LED destination board above the windscreen.
